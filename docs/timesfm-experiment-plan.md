# TimesFM on ZIS Solar: frozen retrospective experiment

Protocol written 12 October 2026, before running TimesFM on the plant.

- Model: `google/timesfm-2.5-200m-pytorch`, Apache 2.0 weights; pin the Hugging Face revision and SHA256. Package `timesfm==2.0.2`, PyTorch 2.8, no fine-tuning. TimesFM 3.0 weights are non-commercial and are not the free commercial deployment candidate.
- Evaluation: every date 21 September–11 October 2026 (21 dates), split into September and October reports. These dates were already inspected in earlier research: **retrospective comparison, not an untouched holdout or proof of prospective improvement**.
- Issue time: preceding day 18:00 UTC, identical to the v3.2 GFS replay. Forecast 28 hourly intervals, retain the 24 delivery hours from local midnight (Europe/Skopje). Hourly energy in kWh, not instantaneous power. No aggregation to three-hour evaluation.
- Context: exactly 240 prior completed hours, latest revision received strictly before the origin; incomplete-at-receipt revisions are excluded. No future target production, late revisions or target-day daily totals enter inference. Save all selected revision IDs, timestamps, missing-value masks and arrays.
- Missing inputs: missing astronomical-night intervals get a explicitly flagged physical zero **for inference only**. Missing daylight gets the median of up to 14 prior same-UTC-hour measured values known at the origin. Require at least 3 values, otherwise omit the fold. Omit a fold if over 10% of its daylight context is imputed. Never fill target actuals; keep real measured outage zeros. Source archives and real readings are never altered.
- Three fixed candidates: (1) TimesFM production history, (2) official XReg + TimesFM with archived GFS irradiance, cloud cover, temperature, wind and deterministic solar geometry/time, ridge=1, separately fitted per issue date, (3) fixed 50:50 mix of candidate 2 with v3.2. No weight search, context search, fine-tuning or retuning after results.
- XReg covariates: `ghi`, `clearSky`, `temperature`, `cloudCover`, `windSpeed`, UTC hour sine and cosine. Historical weather is the archived day-ahead forecast, not realized/reanalysis weather. Every covariate forecast origin precedes the inference origin. Original GFS archive has three-hour source resolution converted by the existing energy-conserving routine; this is a limitation of this like-for-like benchmark, not a claimed new hourly weather source. New native-hourly ICON/GFS/ARPEGE archives do not cover the evaluation range.
- Inference config: max_context=256, requested max_horizon=32 (library rounds internally to its output patch size), batch=1, normalized inputs, flip invariance, positive-series constraint, no quantile head, return_backcast=True (plain forecast uses only the final 28 outputs); torch_compile=False for Windows. CPU PyTorch avoids a 3.5 GB CUDA dependency download; device/effective config and timings are recorded. Save raw outputs. All candidates are clamped to [0, 875] kWh/hour and set to zero only when astronomical clear-sky irradiance is zero. This postprocessing is fixed before scores.
- Baseline: preserved v3.2 causal replay, its original calibration and preceding-day cutoff. Also report causal same-hour persistence as a simple sanity check. Satellite hour-ahead replay has later information and is not the day-ahead competitor.
- Common scoring mask: measured nonnegative complete targets with solar clear-sky irradiance >0 or actual production >0; identical timestamps for every method. Report paired coverage, missing targets, WAPE, MAE, RMSE, signed daily/period MWh error and descriptive max(0,100−WAPE) match score. Never interpret the score as a probability. Main scores retain all actual zeros; September 11/12 confirmed outages are outside the test interval but remain in causal training history.
- Regimes, defined before inference: sunny when the archived daylight mean cloud cover <=30% and range <=40 percentage points; variable when the daylight range >=50 points; otherwise cloudy/mixed. These are forecast-derived diagnostics, not observed sky labels. Report all strata, including small samples.
- Compare daily wins/losses and fixed-seed 2,000-replicate paired daily bootstrap of aggregate WAPE difference (candidate minus baseline). Short sample and serial dependence limit inference; this is exploratory, not a formal promotion test.
- Persistence: append-only `forecast_evaluations` with inputs, source hashes, code, model revision, predictions and metrics. Do not replace CRM history, forecasts, revenue or production model. Promotion requires fresh prospective evidence and a deployable inference service; this local trial adds no cron, paid API or continuous SQL activity.

Sources: https://github.com/google-research/timesfm and https://huggingface.co/google/timesfm-2.5-200m-pytorch .
