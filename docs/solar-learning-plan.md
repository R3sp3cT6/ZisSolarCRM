# Native-hourly site learning — protocol, 12 October 2026

Research question: can a locally trained weather-to-power model beat the existing empirical response, using original hourly irradiance forecasts rather than production-only extrapolation? Written before downloading the new archive or scoring the candidates.

## Market evidence and architectural decision

- SolarAnywhere combines satellite cloud motion at short horizons with location/horizon-dependent NWP blending at longer horizons: https://www.solaranywhere.com/support/forecast-data/model/ .
- Solcast describes NWP bias correction learned against satellite actuals and multiple model inputs; its published improvement is provider/region-specific and cannot be applied as a promised percentage to Resen: https://solcast.com/blog/how-we-improved-day-ahead-solar-forecasts-by-9--with-an-attention-based-model .
- IEA PVPS handbook section9.5 recommends model output statistics, local postprocessing, ensembles and probabilistic forecasts, with careful measurement quality: https://iea-pvps.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Best-Practices-Handbook-for-the-Collection-and-Use-of-Solar-Resource-Data-for-Solar-Energy-Applications-Fourth-Edition-.pdf .
- pvlib provides a transparent weather-to-power chain but needs physical system parameters we do not know. Do not invent panel DC capacity/tilt/azimuth: https://pvlib-python.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/modelchain.html .
- Forecast.Solar requires site configuration; it is not an independent observed-cloud oracle: https://forecast.solar/ . No purchase or paid API.
- NOAA GFS produces original hourly output to120h. We will obtain the original archived fields, not interpolate three-hour temperatures into a claim of hourly irradiance: https://www.emc.ncep.noaa.gov/emc/pages/numerical_forecast_systems/gfs.php .

The new candidate will learn this plant's AC response directly from archived hourly GFS irradiance and Huawei production. The present satellite/Huawei short-horizon trial remains separate. A weather-conditioned supervised model is a different hypothesis from TimesFM or multiplying the existing curve by another manual scalar.

## Fixed experiment

1. Archive prior-day12UTC GFS DSWRF fields for delivery dates16August–11October. Byte-range downloads, original GRIB intervals, run times, Last-Modified, receipts, checksums and decoded Resen point are retained. De-average consecutive interval means into true one-hour irradiation. Require publication before the fixed18UTC issue cutoff. Missing/late archive fields are missing, never reconstructed from future weather.
2. Training at each issue: only prior completed production intervals whose revision was received strictly before that issue, with an original forecast for that interval. Up to45precedingdays; at least120daylight samples spanning10days. No target production, same-day target sum, future revision or outage flag learned after the issue enters fitting. Valid measured zeros remain. No target-day fitting or optimal hindsight shift of the sun curve.
3. Shared deterministic features: GHI, preceding/following hourly GHI from the same known forecast, clear-sky irradiance, clear-sky ratio, solar altitude, sine/cosine of solar azimuth, UTC solar-hour phase, day-of-year phase, forecast daily irradiation, forecast daily maximum. Features use weather forecasts available at issue and known astronomical geometry. No invented geometry or temperature source. Geometry plus learned irradiance response can absorb the plant configuration locally; it does not identify its true hardware.
4. Exactly three candidates before scores: CatBoost direct AC energy (MAE,160iterations,depth4,learning_rate0.05,l2_leaf_reg10,border_count32,random_seed42,bootstrap_typeNo,random_strength0); positive ridge with fixed polynomial interactions (alpha10, standardized features, target kWh/875); nearest weather analog (20neighbors, standardized same features, inverse-distance weights). No parameter grid or repeated tuning on October. Night=0, daytime outputs constrained0–875kWh/h. Save raw outputs and training row IDs per date.
5. Selection: compare rolling causal predictions on21–30September; select the smallest aggregate hourly WAPE, then freeze the selected method before scoring1–11October. All of these dates have been read in prior unrelated experiments: **exploratory retrospective validation, not a virgin holdout**. Report all candidates, all days, the v3.2 original replay, native-hourly GFS with the old plant response (ablation), and TimesFM from its preserved results. Same common daylight mask and no missing targets filled.
6. Metrics: WAPE, MAE/RMSE in kWh, signed MWh error, daily wins, coverage, sample counts and forecast-defined weather regimes. Never promote based on daily energy cancellation or 100−WAPE alone. The confirmed11/12September outages remain in training where known through production; they are not retroactively flagged as available at older origins.
7. Runtime: implement the selected candidate as a bounded separate challenger, preserving v3.2 and the existing comparison table until prospective evidence supports promotion. Freeze/export its weights after fitting only known past data. Add real issued forecasts using the existing collection/cron path, same shared cache and append-only archive, not a new always-on SQL poller. If historical candidates fail, do not hide the failure or ship them as the official forecast.
8. Retain uncertainty and honest horizon labels: day-ahead accuracy is assessed separately from 5–60minute corrections. Generic AI weather forecast resolution cannot guarantee individual cloud-shadow timing. Satellite data observed during a target interval may be used only in a clearly labelled diagnostic, never as a day-ahead predictor.

Data and code will be archived in forecast_evaluations and PROJECT_CONTEXT.md. No plant-control operations, credentials in reports, paid subscriptions or rewrites of original forecasts.

Live collection reuses the available GFS cycle (00/06/12/18UTC); historical fits use12UTC. This lead-time distribution change is recorded and the live challenger is scored independently, rather than assigning the retrospective percentage to the live model. Fitting remains offline and the deployed artifact expires after seven days.
